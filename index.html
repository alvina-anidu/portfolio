Restaurant client

Delish Kitchen & Bar

Sage Hill, Calgary. July 2026 to present.

The brief

Delish is a Nigerian restaurant in Sage Hill. When I took over in July, its Instagram hadn't posted in five weeks. The owner wanted more people coming through the door and a page that felt family friendly.

What I did

The kitchen didn't want any footage of the back-of-house on camera, and most customers would only be filmed without their faces. I built the content around the staff and the tables instead. I captured front-of-house moments, hands-only food shots, and trending formats with the two staff members who were happy to be on camera.

I also modeled hooks on ones that had worked for other North American restaurants in the previous 30 days. The captions asked people to send the post to a friend, since sends were doing more for restaurant posts than tags. I also took over the Facebook page and designed the event flyers in Canva.

What happened

1,942 to 177K views Views in the 33 days before I started, then in my first 90 days. I got these numbers without any ad spend.

Accounts reached went from 185 to 93K. The staff reel I posted on July 27 reached 56K views and 384 shares on its own.

Small business client

Calgary Battery Booster

Calgary. Fall 2026.

The brief

Bard runs Calgary Battery Booster, a mobile jump start and battery service, out of his truck. He booked a one-time shoot for short promo videos.

What I did

I filmed him on location in daylight and after dark and kept him talking to camera in his own words, so the videos sound like him. In the edit I opened each reel on a question a customer would actually ask, then added captions, a name card and an end card with his phone number.

What happened

I delivered two reels. One explains how his business works for potential customers, and the other offers a quick tip on spotting a dying battery.
